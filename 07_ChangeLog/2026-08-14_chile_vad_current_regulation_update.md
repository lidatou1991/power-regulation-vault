# Chile VAD Current Regulation Verification and Update

## Previous Knowledge State

The knowledge base contained a traceable historical account from Olivares (2020). Current cycle, study mechanism, legal basis of [[Empresa Modelo]], active process, and transitional treatment were explicitly **UNKNOWN**.

## New Authoritative Evidence

- [[LGSE DFL 4 Current 2026]] verifies the current general legal framework.
- [[Ley 21.194 Distribution Tariff Reform]] verifies the permanent 2019 procedural reform and temporary freeze.
- [[Ley 21.667 VAD Transitional Measures]] verifies the staged normalization arrangement.
- [[Decreto 5T VAD 2020-2024]] verifies implementation of the single-study process and the latest published formulas.
- [[VAD 2024-2028 Official Process]] and [[Resolucion Exenta 58 2026 VAD 2024-2028]] verify the active process and its preliminary status.
- [[Ministry 2026 VAD Reliquidation Postponement]] records the 2026 postponement of settlement collection without treating the announcement as a permanent rule.

## Current Rule

VAD remains based on efficient model-company costs. The normal tariff-formula term is four years. One CNE-procured and CNE-financed consultant study is supervised by a statutory multi-party committee, processed through participation and dispute stages, reviewed by the CNE, and converted into formulas by Ministry decree. Expired formulas continue until replacement under LGSE article 192, followed by retroactive settlement.

## Historical Rule Preserved

The former parallel CNE and company studies, weighted two-thirds and one-third, remain documented as **HISTORICAL**. The 2016-2020 period and Olivares's contemporaneous description remain intact and linked to the official evidence that superseded the method.

## Transitional Rules

The Law No. 21.194 VAD freeze and Law No. 21.667 staged normalization are classified as **TRANSITIONAL**. Continued use of Decree No. 5T formulas after November 2024 is classified as a statutory carryover pending the 2024-2028 decree, not a new permanent tariff cycle.

## Regulatory and Business Implications

The regulated-cost benchmark is no longer produced by averaging competing CNE and distributor studies. For planning purposes, companies should distinguish the nominal 2024-2028 cost-study period from the formulas currently carried over under Decree No. 5T, and should anticipate article 192 settlement effects once final formulas are published. The timing and operational treatment of remaining 2020-2024 balances remain unresolved in the verified record.
