# v0.3.1 Superseding Law Correction

## Correction

The prior v0.3 wording correctly identified LGSE article 192 as the standing carryover and debit/credit settlement mechanism, but it did not consistently state that Law No. 21.667 later enacted a special condition for the 2020-2024 VAD decree. Article 4 transitory of Law No. 21.667 permits the reliquidations associated with that decree only once the decree is fully processed.

The corrected knowledge model therefore distinguishes:

- the **CURRENT general rule** in LGSE article 192, including continued application of expired formulas pending their replacement; and
- the **TRANSITIONAL special rule** in Law No. 21.667 for the trigger governing reliquidation of the late 2020-2024 decree.

## Preserved Uncertainty

The March 2026 Ministry communication remains evidence of an administrative postponement only. Because no later definitive implementing instrument has been verified, the final collection or credit schedule remains **UNKNOWN** and stays recorded as a research gap.
