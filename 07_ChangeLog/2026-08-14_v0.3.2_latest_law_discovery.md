# v0.3.2 Latest Law Discovery

## Corrected Current-Law Finding

As of 14 August 2026:

- LGSE article 187 retained the normal four-year VAD formula term.
- Decree No. 5T's 2020-2024 formulas remained legally applicable with indexation under LGSE article 192 because replacement formulas had not been published.
- CNE's “VAD 2024-2028” remained a non-final administrative process name.
- Law No. 21.804 was the latest effective LGSE amendment identified; it did not amend articles 181-192.
- Bulletin 18.384-08 had been approved by Congress but remained in presidential approval. Its proposed extension through 2030 and proposed 2028-2035 historical-balance mechanism were not current law at the cutoff.
