# v0.3.4 — Ley No. 21.833 Knowledge Correction

## Previous Incorrect CURRENT Conclusion

v0.3.2 correctly found Bulletin 18.384-08 but incorrectly concluded at the 2026-08-14 cutoff that it remained proposed because the Cámara workflow page still showed presidential approval pending. That led the knowledge base to state 2028-2032 as the next normal period and to classify the 2028-2035 historical-balance mechanism as proposed.

## Root Cause

The verification stopped at stale parliamentary workflow metadata and did not complete the enactment chain through presidential promulgation and Diario Oficial publication. A regulator/process title was also given more legal weight than warranted.

## Decisive Official Evidence

The Diario Oficial published Ley No. 21.833, *Ley de Protección Tarifaria Eléctrica*, in edition No. 44.513, CVE 2847011, on 31 July 2026 after congressional approval on 22 July and presidential promulgation on 24 July. The final enacted text is controlling. Source: [[Ley 21.833 Proteccion Tarifaria Electrica]].

## Corrected Current and Transitional Rules

- **General rule:** VAD formula cycles remain four years.
- **Transitional exception:** the decree originating from the 2024-2028 process must establish formulas for the 2024-2030 sexennium.
- **Legacy name:** “VAD 2024-2028” remains the CNE administrative docket name and is not proof of current legal effect.
- **Next period:** 2030-2034.
- **Covered historical balances:** Ley No. 21.833 provides FET/MPC treatment, SEC determination of Saldo NT, transferable Treasury-issued instruments, a Componente NT beginning at CLP 5/kWh on 1 January 2028, and final payment/extinguishment no later than the last business day of 2035.
- **Delayed 2024-2030 decree:** related balances are accounted for by SEC and incorporated into the 2030-2034 tariff level, with CPI-only adjustment from publication of the law.
