# v0.3.2 Latest Law Discovery — Root-Cause Analysis

## Failure

The v0.3 and v0.3.1 drafts reached authoritative sources but did not independently prove that no later authority changed their legal effect through 14 August 2026.

## Causes

- **Search anchoring:** research began from already stored VAD notes and moved forward instead of searching backward from the cutoff.
- **Source-recency failure:** the existence of a consolidated LGSE copy was treated as sufficient without separately recording its latest amendment and a later-law search.
- **Regulator-page-title anchoring:** the CNE label “VAD 2024-2028” was treated as evidence of the applicable period rather than as the name of an administrative process.
- **Insufficient amendment-history review:** the prior drafts did not expressly test every relied-on law against later amendments, superseding laws, and transitional provisions.
- **Premature stopping:** research stopped after finding authoritative support for the existing model instead of seeking contradictory or more specific 2026 authority.

## Correction

The v0.3.2 review searched 2026 backward from the cutoff, checked the LGSE's latest consolidated amendment, identified Bulletin 18.384-08, and distinguished parliamentary approval from legal effectiveness. The permanent CURRENT-claim and negative-verification rules in `AGENTS.md` are intended to prevent recurrence.
