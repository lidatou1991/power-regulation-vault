01 / 项目是什么？

What Is This?

法规散落在法律、决议、技术标准和网页中；规则不断变化，历史制度与现行制度很容易被混淆。

Normal AI and RAG may retrieve an authoritative-looking passage without proving that it still controls. This project adds legal time, superseding checks and an approval boundary to regulatory memory.

Why Trust It?

当前结论必须经过来源权威、生效日期、修法、后续法律、过渡条款和冲突检查。

✓历史规则永不被静默覆盖。Historical rules are never silently overwritten.

✓人工审核是升级为 CURRENT 的必经闸门。Human Review controls promotion.
