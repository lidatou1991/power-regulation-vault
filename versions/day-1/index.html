01 / THE IDEA

More than retrieval.
A memory with legal time.

Normal chat and RAG can find passages. This experiment also asks whether a rule was amended, superseded, transitional, or still awaiting review—and preserves the answer’s audit trail.

Source-first

Every regulatory conclusion points back to authority.

Time-aware

Historical knowledge is retained when law changes.

Human-gated

Research candidates cannot promote themselves to CURRENT.

03 / HOW IT LEARNS

LLM 理解，Wiki 记忆，人工判断。

一位非技术管理者可以在 30 秒内看懂的现行法可信流程。

  1. 01Professional Literature + Official Regulatory Sources专业文献提供基础，权威来源控制现行结论
  2. 02LLM Extraction & ReasoningRules, dates and conflicts are identified
  3. 03Atomic Wiki KnowledgeFocused nodes connect into a reusable network
  4. 04Temporal StatusCURRENT · HISTORICAL · TRANSITIONAL · PROPOSED
  5. 05Superseding CheckLater authority is searched in reverse chronology
  6. 06Change CandidateA claim-level evidence package is prepared
  7. 07Human ReviewThe mandatory promotion gate
  8. 08Verified CURRENT KnowledgeOnly approved claims enter the trusted layer
  9. 09Benchmark + Continuous LearningCapability is measured and gaps drive the next cycle

07 / THREE-LAYER INTELLIGENCE

为什么是三个分数？

48.22 与 69.90 之间的差距是有意设计的安全边界，而不是弱点。

08 / DAILY SELF-GROWTH

每天，它都留下自己成长的证据。

Daily Learning Brief 在每个研究日记录：学到了什么、改变和纠正了什么、仍不确定什么、知识指标、基准变化与下一步优先级。公开网站只展示安全的汇总信息，不公开内部研究缺口或私人审核材料。

09 / ANTI-HALLUCINATION

Confidence is not authority.

Before a CURRENT conclusion, the system checks authority, effective date, amendments, superseding law, transitions and conflicts.

✓Historical rules are never silently overwritten.
✓NEEDS_REVIEW work stays outside approved capability.
✓A negative later-law search records its verification cutoff.
