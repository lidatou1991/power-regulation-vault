04 / 核心理念

Core Concept

LLM负责阅读、比较与推理；Wiki保存来源、时间状态、冲突和关系；Human Review决定什么可以进入正式 CURRENT 层。

Unlike ordinary chat or retrieval, the system preserves historical rules, checks later authority, exposes uncertainty and blocks self-approval.

FOUNDATION KNOWLEDGE

Historical and conceptual understanding; not current law.

EVIDENCE READY

Claim-level packages ready for review; not approved.

APPROVED CURRENT

Only human-reviewed conclusions enter this layer.
