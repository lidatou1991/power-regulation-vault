## VAD-Relevant Scope

The decree completes the 2020-2024 process under Law No. 21.194 and records a single CNE-procured consultant study, committee supervision, public observations, and Panel de Expertos review. Although its named period ended in November 2024, LGSE article 192 keeps the formulas and indexation in force until replacement formulas are published; the replacement then applies from the prior period's expiry with debit/credit settlement. For the distinct reliquidation generated when this late 2020-2024 decree was issued, Law No. 21.667 article 4 transitory supplies the later, special rule and permits reliquidation only after the decree is fully processed.
