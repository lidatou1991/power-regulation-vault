# Ley 21.194 Distribution Tariff Reform

## Document Information

- **Official title:** Ley No. 21.194, Rebaja la rentabilidad de las empresas de distribución y perfecciona el proceso tarifario de distribución eléctrica
- **Institution:** Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional de Chile (LeyChile); Ministerio de Energía
- **Publication/effective date:** 21 December 2019
- **Official URL:** https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?idNorma=1140301
- **Retrieved:** 2026-08-14

## VAD-Relevant Scope

The law amended the LGSE's distribution-tariff provisions. It introduced the current discount-rate range, replaced the former parallel CNE/company studies with one CNE-procured study, and established the technical committee, public-participation, observation, and Panel de Expertos stages now reflected in LGSE articles 182 bis, 183, and 183 bis.

Article 13 transitory froze VAD price levels in pesos, creating a temporary regime later addressed by Law No. 21.667.

## Temporal Treatment

The procedural amendments are part of the current consolidated LGSE. The price freeze was **TRANSITIONAL**, not a replacement of the normal four-year rule.

## Later-Authority Check

The permanent procedural amendments were checked against the LGSE consolidated through Law No. 21.804 and later legislation through 14 August 2026. Law No. 21.667 superseded the temporary freeze treatment. Ley No. 21.833 then enacted further transitional VAD-period and balance measures without restoring the historical weighted-study mechanism.
