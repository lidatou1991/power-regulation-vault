## VAD-Relevant Scope

Articles 181-192 were reviewed. In particular, articles 182 and 182 bis define VAD components and the discount-rate framework; articles 183 and 183 bis govern the model-company cost study and participatory procedure; article 187 establishes the normal four-year term; article 190 assigns tariff-formula issuance to the Ministry of Energy; and article 192 preserves expired formulas until replacement and requires later debit/credit settlement.
