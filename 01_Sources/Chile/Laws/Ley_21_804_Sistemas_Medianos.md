## Legal Role in the VAD Review

LeyChile identifies Law No. 21.804 as the latest amendment incorporated into the LGSE consolidated version effective 13 February 2026. The law principally reforms medium-system definitions, planning, generation and transmission pricing. Its amendment to LGSE article 152 updates the systems covered by the existing minimum-profitability provision. It does **not** amend LGSE articles 181-192, the provisions governing national distribution VAD components, the model-company study, the four-year formula term, the tariff decree, or carryover and reliquidation.

Accordingly, it is the latest effective LGSE amendment checked at the cutoff, but it does not supersede the current national VAD-cycle rules established in LGSE articles 181-192 or the period-specific transitional treatment in Law No. 21.667.
