# Ley 21.667 VAD Transitional Measures

## Document Information

- **Official title:** Ley No. 21.667, Modifica diversos cuerpos legales, en materia de estabilización tarifaria
- **Institution:** Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional de Chile (LeyChile); Ministerio de Energía
- **Publication/effective date:** 30 April 2024
- **Official URL:** https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?idNorma=1203005
- **Retrieved:** 2026-08-14

## VAD-Relevant Scope

Article 4 transitory ended the Law No. 21.194 VAD freeze in stages: immediately for cooperatives in connection with the first-semester 2024 average-node-price decree, with capped adjustments for other distributors during 2024 and the first half of 2025, and normal updating from the second-semester 2025 decree. It also directed use of the 2020-2024 VAD decree formulas, when published, together with the settlements required by LGSE article 192.

For the decree fixing VAD for 2020-2024, the same provision adds a special timing rule: the statutory reliquidations may occur only once that decree is fully processed (*totalmente tramitado*). As the later and more specific enactment for this tariff period, Law No. 21.667 controls that settlement timing; LGSE article 192 remains the general rule for expired formulas and later debit/credit settlement.

## Temporal Treatment

These provisions are **TRANSITIONAL** tariff-normalization measures. They do not alter the **CURRENT** general four-year term in LGSE article 187. Their special 2020-2024 settlement condition must, however, be read before the general rule in LGSE article 192 when describing reliquidation of that period.

## Later-Authority Check

Ley No. 21.833, published on 31 July 2026, is later authority for the covered historical balances. It places the relevant debts within the FET/MPC mechanism and establishes SEC determination, Saldo NT, Treasury-issued payment instruments, the Componente NT, and the 2035 deadline. Law No. 21.667 remains historical and operative only to the extent not modified or displaced. Sources: [[Ley 21.833 Proteccion Tarifaria Electrica]]; [[Boletin 18.384-08 Proteccion Tarifaria]].
