## VAD Period Transition

The third transitional article requires the tariff-formula decree corresponding to the 2024-2028 quadriennium to establish formulas instead for the **2024-2030 six-year period**. The procedure continues using the cost study, preliminary technical report, Resolution No. 222/2023 bases, and observations from the process originally constituted for 2024-2028. Later CNE technical reports must implement the extension.

This is a transitional exception. It does not repeal the normal four-year term in LGSE article 187. The fourth transitional article expressly identifies the next VAD process as the **2030-2034 quadriennium**.

## Historical Reliquidation Balances

Article 1(8) expands the FET's purposes to payment of debts from the relevant LGSE articles 178 and 192 reliquidations for the 2020-2024 and 2022-2026 processes. Article 2 amends Law No. 21.472 as follows:

- SEC determines amounts actually owed to each distributor, in pesos, producing the **Saldo de Normalización Tarifaria (Saldo NT)**.
- On instruction from the Ministry of Finance, the Treasury issues each electrical company a transferable payment instrument in pesos or UF. Total payment may not be later than the last business day of 2035.
- The Cargo MPC comprises the Componente Saldo Final Restante and the **Componente NT**.
- The Componente NT starts at **CLP 5/kWh**, is adjusted semiannually as specified by the law, applies from **1 January 2028**, and continues until the Saldo NT is extinguished, no later than the last business day of 2035. CNE must adjust it if necessary for financial sufficiency.
- The second transitional article requires SEC's report within forty business days of publication, supported by specified submissions from electrical companies and CNE.

## Balances from the Delayed 2024-2030 Decree

The fourth transitional article separately provides that amounts owed to distributors because of delayed publication of the 2024-2030 decree are accounted for by SEC and incorporated into each distributor's tariff level in the **2030-2034** VAD process. From publication of Ley No. 21.833, those reliquidations are adjusted only by CPI.
