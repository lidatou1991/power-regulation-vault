## VAD-Relevant Scope

The resolution and attached report confirm that the 2024-2028 study was performed by the CNE-contracted consultant INECON for twelve Typical Distribution Areas, reviewed by the CNE, and remained at the preliminary-report/observations stage on the resolution date. It records 878 observations to the earlier preliminary report and the opening of a renewed observation stage.

This source supports the status of the latest process. It does not itself constitute the final tariff decree.
