## Scope and Current Procedural Evidence

The page is the CNE's official docket for the 2024-2028 VAD process. It records the Typical Distribution Areas, public-participation registry, final technical bases, consultant procurement, technical committee, consultant's final cost study, and CNE reports.

The latest listed substantive milestone reviewed is Resolution No. 58 of 30 January 2026, which rescinded Resolution No. 626/2025, approved a new preliminary technical report, and opened a new observation stage. The page did not list a final technical report or tariff decree for 2024-2028 as of retrieval. **Status: CURRENT PROCESS; not yet final.**

## Legacy Name and Later-Law Check

“VAD 2024-2028” is now a legacy administrative name, not proof of the formulas' current legal period. Ley No. 21.833, published on 31 July 2026, requires this process to establish formulas for 2024-2030. See [[Ley 21.833 Proteccion Tarifaria Electrica]] and [[VAD 2024-2028 Legacy Name and Legal Effect]].
