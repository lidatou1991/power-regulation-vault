# Ministry 2026 Ordenemos la Cuenta

## Document Information

- **Official page title:** Por amplia mayoría, el Congreso despacha a Ley proyecto “Ordenemos la Cuenta”
- **Institution:** Ministerio de Energía
- **Publication date:** 23 July 2026
- **Official URL:** https://energia.gob.cl/noticias/nacional/extension-del-subsidio-y-mejoras-en-distribucion-por-amplia-mayoria-el-congreso-despacha-ley-proyecto-ordenemos-la-cuenta
- **Retrieved:** 14 August 2026

## Scope

The Ministry describes the congressionally approved protection-tariff bill. For VAD, it states that the initiative would address balances from the delayed 2020-2024 process through a gradual mechanism operating from 2028 to 2035. The bill's official Cámara record separately links the legislative text and is preserved in [[Boletin 18.384-08 Proteccion Tarifaria]].

## Authority Limit

The page uses political shorthand that the project was “dispatched as law,” but it is not the Diario Oficial enactment. The official Cámara status record still showed presidential approval pending at the cutoff. This page therefore supports the content of the pending proposal, not a **CURRENT** legal conclusion.
