# Ministry 2026 VAD Reliquidation Postponement

## Document Information

- **Official title:** Ministra Rincón por aplazamiento del alza en cuentas de la luz: “Nuestro foco son las personas y evitarles más dolores en el día a día”
- **Institution:** Ministerio de Energía
- **Publication date:** 30 March 2026
- **Effective date:** not applicable; official communication concerning an administrative postponement
- **Official URL:** https://energia.gob.cl/node/27208
- **Retrieved:** 2026-08-14

## VAD-Relevant Scope

The Ministry stated that collection of 2020-2024 VAD tariff settlements, then expected to begin on 1 April 2026, had been postponed, with 1 July 2026 identified as the outside date at that time. This is evidence of a temporary implementation event, not authority for changing the normal VAD cycle or the LGSE article 192 settlement principle.

No later definitive settlement instrument was verified during this ingestion. The final payment/credit schedule therefore remains an open research question.
