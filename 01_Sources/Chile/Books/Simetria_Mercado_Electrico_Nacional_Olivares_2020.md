## VAD-Related Scope Reviewed

Only Chapter 6, *Mercado de la Distribución*, was reviewed for this ingestion. The directly relevant range was:

- Chapter introduction and §6.1 for the distribution-market and historical context (pp. 617-621).
- §6.3 on natural monopoly and density economies (pp. 634-635).
- §6.4 on the price-cap framework (pp. 636-638).
- §6.5-6.5.2.5 on distribution pricing, VAD, Typical Distribution Areas, [[Empresa Modelo]], VAD components, preliminary tariffs, and the profitability check (pp. 638-657).
- §6.5.3-6.5.3.2 on the author's description of Law No. 21.194 and its intended tariff-study changes (pp. 658-662).
- §6.6 on the 2016-2020 quadrennial process (pp. 667-669).
- The opening of §6.7 on the relationship between VAD and final distribution-level tariffs (pp. 669-671).

Other chapters and unrelated portions of Chapter 6 were not ingested.

## Use in the Knowledge Base

This book supplies the first sourced historical account for Chilean [[VAD]], [[Empresa Modelo]], distribution pricing, the tariff-cycle structure described in 2020, and the [[VAD Regulatory Timeline]]. It is used to identify historical mechanisms and research questions that require later verification from laws, decrees, and official CNE materials.

## Historical-Reference Limitation

This is a secondary source published in January 2020. Statements extracted from it are classified as `historical` or `unknown`; they are not treated as evidence of the rules currently in force. Descriptions of Law No. 21.194 and the anticipated 2020-2024 process record the author's publication-time account and require authoritative verification.
