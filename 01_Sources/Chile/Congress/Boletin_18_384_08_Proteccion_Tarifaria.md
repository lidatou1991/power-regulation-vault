## Legal Status Correction

Congress approved the bill and sent it to the Executive on 22 July 2026. It was promulgated on 24 July and published as Ley No. 21.833 in Diario Oficial No. 44.513, CVE 2847011, on 31 July 2026. The bill record is therefore **HISTORICAL**; the enacted law is **CURRENT**. Source: [[Ley 21.833 Proteccion Tarifaria Electrica]].

The Cámara workflow page still displayed *Trámite de aprobación presidencial* when checked on 14 August. This conflicts with the controlling Diario Oficial enactment and is preserved as stale workflow metadata. It must not be used to downgrade the law to proposed status.

The final enacted wording is verified from the Diario Oficial, not inferred from the bill summary or the inaccessible congressional attachment.

## Relationship to Existing Rules

Ley No. 21.833 is the later, more specific enacted authority for the 2024-2030 transition and the covered historical balances. The normal LGSE four-year cycle and Decree No. 5T carryover remain relevant where the new law does not displace them.
