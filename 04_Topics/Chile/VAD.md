# VAD

## Current Rule

Current status not yet verified from an authoritative current source.

The 2020 book is a historical reference. It cannot establish which VAD rules, tariff cycle, methodology, or decree are currently effective.

## Historical Rules

### Regulatory design described for 1982-2019

Olivares describes distribution as a natural monopoly regulated through an adapted price-cap model. Under that historical design, the Valor Agregado de Distribución (VAD) represented efficient distribution costs rather than each distributor's actual costs. The model sought to remunerate efficient investment, operation, maintenance, administration, customer service, and recognized distribution losses. **Status: historical.** Source: [[Simetria Mercado Electrico Nacional Olivares 2020|Olivares (2020)]], §§6.3-6.5.2, pp. 634-647.

The book identifies three groups of VAD components under that framework: fixed costs for administration, billing, and customer service; average power and energy loss factors; and standard investment, operation, and maintenance costs per unit of supplied power. Distribution costs were differentiated for high- and low-voltage networks. **Status: historical.** Source: [[Simetria Mercado Electrico Nacional Olivares 2020|Olivares (2020)]], §6.5.2.3, pp. 646-650.

For tariff periods before the 2019 short distribution law took effect, the book describes separate cost studies by the CNE and the distribution companies. Their results were weighted two-thirds for the CNE study and one-third for the company study. The book states that this mechanism was used for tariff periods from 1982 through the 2016-2020 period. **Status: historical.** Source: [[Simetria Mercado Electrico Nacional Olivares 2020|Olivares (2020)]], §§6.5.2 and 6.6, pp. 642-643 and 667-669.

The historical process grouped distributors into Typical Distribution Areas (A.T.D.) with similar average costs and selected a reference company for each area. Efficient costs were then calculated through the [[Empresa Modelo]] methodology. **Status: historical.** Source: [[Simetria Mercado Electrico Nacional Olivares 2020|Olivares (2020)]], §§6.5.2.1-6.5.2.3, pp. 644-647.

The book characterizes the VAD tariff-setting process as quadrennial and states that the resulting decree fixed distribution components and regulated-customer tariff options for four years. This is a report of the framework described in a 2020 publication, not verification of the cycle currently in force. **Status: historical.** Source: [[Simetria Mercado Electrico Nacional Olivares 2020|Olivares (2020)]], §§6.5 and 6.5.2, pp. 638-643.

### 2016-2020 tariff period

For the 2016-2020 period, the book reports six Typical Distribution Areas, one reference company for each area, and use of the weighted CNE-company studies. It identifies Decree 11T of 2016 as the original tariff decree and Decree 5T of 2018 as an update reflecting revised service-quality and metering-related costs. **Status: historical.** Source: [[Simetria Mercado Electrico Nacional Olivares 2020|Olivares (2020)]], §6.6, pp. 667-669.

### 2019 short distribution law as described in 2020

The book reports that Law No. 21.194, promulgated in December 2019, replaced the two-study weighting mechanism with one independently procured cost study supervised through a committee process, while retaining efficiency and model-company assumptions and adding consideration of constraints faced by real distributors. The book presents the 2020-2024 process as the first application of this change. **Status: historical account; subsequent implementation and continued validity unknown.** Source: [[Simetria Mercado Electrico Nacional Olivares 2020|Olivares (2020)]], §§6.5.3-6.5.3.2, pp. 658-662.

## Transitional Rules

Olivares describes the 2020-2024 tariff process as the intended first application of Law No. 21.194 and notes that a broader distribution reform was expected. Whether these provisions operated as described, were extended, replaced, or remain relevant has not been verified from official sources. **Status: unknown.** Source: [[Simetria Mercado Electrico Nacional Olivares 2020|Olivares (2020)]], §6.5.3.2, pp. 660-662.
