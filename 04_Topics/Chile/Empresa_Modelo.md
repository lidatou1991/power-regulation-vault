# Empresa Modelo

## Current Rule

Empresa Modelo remains the legal efficiency benchmark for calculating [[VAD]]. LGSE article 182 defines the VAD cost categories by reference to model companies, while article 183 requires the cost study to assume efficient investment and management by a model company for each Typical Distribution Area. **Status: CURRENT.** Source: [[LGSE DFL 4 Current 2026]], arts. 182-183.

The LGSE amendment history and later VAD legislation were checked through 14 August 2026. Law No. 21.804 did not amend articles 182-183. Ley No. 21.833 added a new item to article 183 and created the 2024-2030 transition but did not displace Empresa Modelo. **Negative verification: completed.** Sources: [[Ley 21.804 Sistemas Medianos]]; [[Ley 21.833 Proteccion Tarifaria Electrica]].

The model is not a reimbursement of each distributor's actual expenditure. It calculates efficient standard costs while incorporating legally specified constraints affecting real distributors: the location of customers and demand, applicable quality rules, road layout and obstacles, technology penetration, changes in service quality, vegetation, and other constraints expressly identified in the study bases. **Status: CURRENT.** Source: [[LGSE DFL 4 Current 2026]], art. 183.

The current process uses one independent consultant study commissioned and financed by the CNE, supervised by the statutory technical committee. Resolution No. 58/2026 confirms application of this framework to twelve Typical Distribution Areas in the 2024-2028 process. **Status: CURRENT PROCESS / preliminary.** Sources: [[LGSE DFL 4 Current 2026]], arts. 183-183 bis; [[Resolucion Exenta 58 2026 VAD 2024-2028]].

## Historical Rules

For the historical VAD method described by Olivares, the CNE selected one reference distributor for each Typical Distribution Area. Selection considered technical-economic criteria such as demand served and practical criteria such as the availability and reliability of information needed for the cost study. **Status: historical.** Source: [[Simetria Mercado Electrico Nacional Olivares 2020|Olivares (2020)]], §6.5.2.2, pp. 645-646.

The model company began from characteristics of the reference company - including its operating territory, customers, consumption, and injection points - but modeled an efficient network adapted to demand and an efficient investment and management policy. Its referential works plan was not necessarily the real distributor's investment plan. **Status: historical.** Source: [[Simetria Mercado Electrico Nacional Olivares 2020|Olivares (2020)]], §§6.5.2.2 and 6.5.4, pp. 645-646 and 666-667.

Under this historical approach, distribution tariffs reflected efficient costs calculated for the model company rather than each distributor's actual costs. Information from real companies was nevertheless used for the industry profitability check. **Status: historical.** Source: [[Simetria Mercado Electrico Nacional Olivares 2020|Olivares (2020)]], §§6.5-6.5.2.5, pp. 638-657.

The model-company VAD described in the book included fixed customer-service costs, average power and energy losses, and standard investment, maintenance, and operating costs for facilities adapted to demand. **Status: historical.** Source: [[Simetria Mercado Electrico Nacional Olivares 2020|Olivares (2020)]], §6.5.2.3, pp. 646-650.

Olivares reports that Law No. 21.194 retained model-company efficiency assumptions for the cost-study method intended for the 2020-2024 process, while requiring consideration of certain real-world constraints such as customer location, road layout, technology penetration, quality standards, and vegetation. **Status: HISTORICAL ACCOUNT, now verified as reflected in current LGSE article 183.** Sources: [[Simetria Mercado Electrico Nacional Olivares 2020|Olivares (2020)]], §6.5.3.2, pp. 660-662; [[LGSE DFL 4 Current 2026]].

## Transitional Rules

The book's anticipated first application of Law No. 21.194 occurred in the 2020-2024 process culminating in Decree No. 5T. The one-study framework and Empresa Modelo were carried into the 2024-2028 process. **Status: HISTORICAL TRANSITION COMPLETED; framework CURRENT.** Sources: [[Decreto 5T VAD 2020-2024]]; [[VAD 2024-2028 Official Process]].
