# VAD Regulatory Timeline

This timeline preserves the historical account from Olivares (2020) and adds official post-publication evidence. A **general rule** is distinguished from a **temporary/transitional exception**.

## Before 2020

### 1982: model-company framework

Olivares identifies 1982 as the origin of the VAD distribution tariff framework based on an adapted price-cap design and [[Empresa Modelo]]. **Status: HISTORICAL.** Source: [[Simetria Mercado Electrico Nacional Olivares 2020|Olivares (2020)]], Chapter 6 introduction and §§6.4-6.5.2, pp. 617-647.

### 1982-2019: parallel-study methodology

The book describes a quadrennial process using separate CNE and distributor cost studies, weighted two-thirds and one-third respectively. **Status: HISTORICAL; superseded for later processes.** Source: [[Simetria Mercado Electrico Nacional Olivares 2020|Olivares (2020)]], §6.5.2, pp. 642-647.

### 2015-2018: 2016-2020 process and update

Olivares records Resolution No. 699/2015 and Resolution No. 79/2016 as the bases, Decree No. 11T/2016 as the tariff decree, and Decree No. 5T/2018 as an update for new quality and metering costs. **Status: HISTORICAL.** Source: [[Simetria Mercado Electrico Nacional Olivares 2020|Olivares (2020)]], §6.6, pp. 667-669.

### 21 December 2019: Law No. 21.194

Law No. 21.194 replaced the two-study weighting with one CNE-procured study, added a technical committee and participation/dispute stages, and changed the discount-rate and profitability framework. These procedural changes became the **CURRENT general rule** now consolidated in LGSE articles 182 bis, 183, and 183 bis. Sources: [[Ley 21.194 Distribution Tariff Reform]]; [[LGSE DFL 4 Current 2026]].

The same law's article 13 transitory froze VAD price levels in pesos. **Status: TRANSITIONAL exception, not a change to the four-year cycle.** Source: [[Ley 21.194 Distribution Tariff Reform]].

## 2020-2024

### 2020-2023: first process under the reformed method

The 2020-2024 process used one independent consultant study (INECON), supervised through the statutory committee and subject to participation and Panel review. The consultant's final study was delivered in 2022 and the CNE's final technical work concluded later, evidencing a material delay. **Status: HISTORICAL IMPLEMENTATION.** Source: [[Decreto 5T VAD 2020-2024]].

### 30 April 2024: Law No. 21.667

Law No. 21.667 established a staged exit from the VAD freeze: special treatment for cooperatives, capped adjustments for other distributors in the second half of 2024 and first half of 2025, and return to normal updating from the second-semester 2025 price decree. For the forthcoming 2020-2024 decree, it also enacted the later, special condition that reliquidations could occur only after the decree was fully processed. That condition controls the timing for this period; LGSE article 192 remains the general settlement rule. **Status: TRANSITIONAL.** Source: [[Ley 21.667 VAD Transitional Measures]].

### 2024-2025: Decree No. 5T for 2020-2024

Decree No. 5T was promulgated on 31 May 2024, exceptionally published on 7 June 2024, and published after full processing on 21 April 2025. It fixes formulas for 4 November 2020-3 November 2024 and formally implements the single-study framework. **Status: HISTORICAL TARIFF PERIOD / CURRENT CARRYOVER FORMULAS.** Source: [[Decreto 5T VAD 2020-2024]].

## Original 2024-2028 Process and Enacted 2024-2030 Transition

### 2022-2023: process preparation

The CNE fixed the Typical Distribution Areas in 2022, constituted the citizen-participation registry, approved final technical bases in 2023, procured the consultant, and constituted the technical committee. **Status: HISTORICAL PROCEDURAL MILESTONES.** Source: [[VAD 2024-2028 Official Process]].

### 2025-30 January 2026: preliminary reports

The CNE issued Preliminary Technical Report Resolution No. 626/2025, then rescinded it and approved a replacement report through Resolution No. 58/2026 after 878 observations, opening a renewed observation stage. **Status: CURRENT PROCESS / PRELIMINARY.** Sources: [[VAD 2024-2028 Official Process]]; [[Resolucion Exenta 58 2026 VAD 2024-2028]].

### 13 February 2026: Law No. 21.804

Law No. 21.804 became effective and is the latest amendment shown in the LGSE consolidated version at the cutoff. Its official text principally reforms medium systems and does not amend LGSE articles 181-192 governing the national VAD study, four-year term, tariff decree, carryover, or reliquidation. **Status: CURRENT; latest effective amendment checked, not a superseding VAD-period rule.** Sources: [[Ley 21.804 Sistemas Medianos]]; [[LGSE DFL 4 Current 2026]].

### 22 June-22 July 2026: Bulletin 18.384-08 legislative phase

The Executive introduced the protection-tariff bill on 22 June. Congress approved and sent it to the Executive on 22 July. **Status: HISTORICAL LEGISLATIVE PHASE; superseded by enactment.** Source: [[Boletin 18.384-08 Proteccion Tarifaria]].

### 24 July 2026: presidential promulgation

The President promulgated the bill as Ley No. 21.833. **Status: HISTORICAL ENACTMENT MILESTONE.** Source: [[Ley 21.833 Proteccion Tarifaria Electrica]].

### 31 July 2026: Diario Oficial publication and legal effect

Ley No. 21.833 was published in Diario Oficial No. 44.513, CVE 2847011, and became generally effective. Its third transitional article changed the legal output of the original process from 2024-2028 to the **2024-2030 sexennium**, while retaining the existing study and procedural materials. Its fourth transitional article identifies the next period as the **2030-2034 quadriennium**. **Status: CURRENT TRANSITIONAL LAW.** Source: [[Ley 21.833 Proteccion Tarifaria Electrica]].

### 1 January 2028: Componente NT starts

The Componente NT of the Cargo MPC begins at CLP 5/kWh, subject to the law's adjustment rules, and continues until the Saldo NT is extinguished no later than the last business day of 2035. **Status: FUTURE OPERATION OF CURRENT ENACTED RULE.** Source: [[Ley 21.833 Proteccion Tarifaria Electrica]], article 2(5).

### 2030-2034: next normal four-year VAD period

The next VAD tariff period is 2030-2034. The normal four-year logic continues thereafter unless later law changes it. Balances due to delayed publication of the 2024-2030 decree are incorporated into the 2030-2034 tariff level under the fourth transitional article. **Status: CURRENT ENACTED FUTURE PERIOD.** Source: [[Ley 21.833 Proteccion Tarifaria Electrica]].

### Current status at 14 August 2026

- **General rule — CURRENT:** tariff formulas have a four-year term; the study is a single CNE-procured consultant study based on Empresa Modelo; the Ministry fixes final formulas by decree. Source: [[LGSE DFL 4 Current 2026]], arts. 183, 183 bis, 187 and 190.
- **Transitional exception — CURRENT:** Ley No. 21.833 requires formulas for 2024-2030; the next period is 2030-2034. Source: [[Ley 21.833 Proteccion Tarifaria Electrica]].
- **Legacy process name — NOT LEGAL EFFECT:** the CNE docket retained the label 2024-2028 and remained at the replacement preliminary-report/observations stage in the latest official materials reviewed. Sources: [[VAD 2024-2028 Official Process]]; [[Resolucion Exenta 58 2026 VAD 2024-2028]]; [[VAD 2024-2028 Legacy Name and Legal Effect]].
- **Carryover — CURRENT statutory mechanism:** Decree No. 5T's 2020-2024 formulas continue with indexation until replacement; the new formulas will apply from 4 November 2024 with debit/credit settlement. Sources: [[LGSE DFL 4 Current 2026]], art. 192; [[Decreto 5T VAD 2020-2024]].
- **Freeze normalization — TRANSITIONAL:** Law No. 21.667's staged measures are separate from the permanent cycle. Source: [[Ley 21.667 VAD Transitional Measures]].
- **Historical balances — CURRENT TRANSITIONAL MECHANISM:** Ley No. 21.833 establishes FET/MPC treatment, SEC calculation of Saldo NT, transferable Treasury payment instruments, and the Componente NT through no later than the last business day of 2035. Source: [[Ley 21.833 Proteccion Tarifaria Electrica]].
