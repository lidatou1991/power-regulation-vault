# VAD Regulatory Timeline

This timeline records only the historical stages described by Olivares in 2020. It does not establish the law or tariff framework currently in force.

## Before 2020

### 1982

Olivares identifies 1982 as the origin of the VAD distribution tariff framework based on an adapted price-cap design and [[Empresa Modelo]]. **Status: historical.** Source: [[Simetria Mercado Electrico Nacional Olivares 2020|Olivares (2020)]], Chapter 6 introduction and §§6.4-6.5.2, pp. 617-647.

### 1982-2019 methodology

The book describes a quadrennial process using separate CNE and distributor cost studies, weighted two-thirds and one-third respectively, to determine VAD components and distribution tariffs. **Status: historical.** Source: [[Simetria Mercado Electrico Nacional Olivares 2020|Olivares (2020)]], §6.5.2, pp. 642-647.

### 2015-2016: start of the 2016-2020 process

According to the book, Resolution No. 699 of 2015 provided preliminary technical bases and Typical Distribution Areas; Resolution No. 79 of February 2016 incorporated changes and fixed the definitive bases. Decree 11T of November 2016 then set tariffs for the 2016-2020 period. **Status: historical.** Source: [[Simetria Mercado Electrico Nacional Olivares 2020|Olivares (2020)]], §6.6, pp. 667-668.

### 2017-2018: update within the 2016-2020 period

The book reports that new quality and metering requirements led the CNE and distributors to agree to a new VAD study through Resolution No. 560 of 2017. It identifies Resolution No. 173 of March 2018 as approving the updated technical report and Decree 5T, published on 28 September 2018, as updating tariff formulas through the end of the 2016-2020 period. **Status: historical.** Source: [[Simetria Mercado Electrico Nacional Olivares 2020|Olivares (2020)]], §6.6, pp. 668-669.

### 2019: Law No. 21.194

Olivares reports that Law No. 21.194 was promulgated on 21 December 2019. The book describes it as replacing the historical CNE-company study weighting with one independent cost study process and changing the discount-rate and profitability-review framework. **Status: historical account.** Source: [[Simetria Mercado Electrico Nacional Olivares 2020|Olivares (2020)]], §6.5.3, pp. 658-662.

## 2020

At publication, the author described the 2020-2024 VAD process as the intended first application of Law No. 21.194 and anticipated a broader reform of distribution regulation. This records the author's 2020 description only; later implementation has not been checked. **Status: unknown.** Source: [[Simetria Mercado Electrico Nacional Olivares 2020|Olivares (2020)]], §§6.5.3.1-6.5.3.2, pp. 659-662.
