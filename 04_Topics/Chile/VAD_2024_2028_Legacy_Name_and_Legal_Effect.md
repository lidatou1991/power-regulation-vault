# VAD 2024-2028 Legacy Name and Legal Effect

## Legacy Administrative / Process Name

CNE continues to title its docket **“VAD 2024-2028 y Servicios Asociados.”** The name identifies the cost-study and tariff-setting process as originally initiated. As of 14 August 2026, the reviewed CNE docket had not listed a final technical report or final tariff decree. **Status: LEGACY ADMINISTRATIVE PROCESS NAME / PROCEDURE NOT FINAL.** Sources: [[VAD 2024-2028 Official Process]]; [[Resolucion Exenta 58 2026 VAD 2024-2028]].

## Current Legal Effect at 14 August 2026

**GENERAL RULE:** the statutory formula term remains four years under LGSE article 187, and expired formulas carry over under article 192 until replacements are published. **Status: CURRENT.** Sources: [[LGSE DFL 4 Current 2026]], arts. 187, 190 and 192; [[Decreto 5T VAD 2020-2024]].

**TRANSITIONAL EXCEPTION:** Ley No. 21.833's third transitional article requires the decree corresponding to the original 2024-2028 process to set formulas instead for the six-year period **2024-2030**. Existing study materials and observations remain part of that procedure. This enacted exception has controlled since publication on 31 July 2026. **Status: CURRENT TRANSITIONAL LAW.** Source: [[Ley 21.833 Proteccion Tarifaria Electrica]].

**LEGACY ADMINISTRATIVE PROCESS NAME:** the CNE webpage title remains “VAD 2024-2028.” It is not proof that the final formulas' current legal period ends in 2028. Source: [[VAD 2024-2028 Official Process]].

## Reason for the Apparent Discrepancy

The CNE docket name predates Ley No. 21.833 and records the period for which the administrative process originated. The official Cámara workflow page also remained at presidential approval after enactment. The Diario Oficial publication controls both stale labels. This source conflict is preserved in [[Boletin 18.384-08 Proteccion Tarifaria]] rather than silently corrected.

## Next Period

Under the enacted transition, the current exceptional period is **2024-2030** and the next tariff period is the normal four-year period **2030-2034**. The general four-year logic then continues unless a later law changes it. Sources: [[Ley 21.833 Proteccion Tarifaria Electrica]], third, fourth, and sixteenth transitional articles.
